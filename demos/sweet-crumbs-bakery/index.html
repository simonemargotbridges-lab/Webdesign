Our Story

From Our Kitchen to Your Table

Sweet Crumbs started in 2016 as a farmers market booth in downtown Wellesley. After two years of selling out every weekend, we opened our bakery at 45 Center Street — and we haven't looked back.

We believe good baking takes patience. Our croissants take three days. Our sourdough takes 24 hours. We don't cut corners, and you can taste the difference.

  • Locally sourced ingredients from New England farms
  • No artificial preservatives or additives
  • Seasonal menu that changes with the harvest
  • Custom cakes available for every occasion
