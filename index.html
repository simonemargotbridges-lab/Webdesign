About Me

Hi, I'm Simone Bridges.

Web designer, problem-solver, your business's biggest fan.

I'm a senior at Dana Hall School in Wellesley, and this fall I'll be heading to NYU. I've always believed that every local business — no matter how small — deserves a professional online presence. A great website isn't a luxury. It's how customers find you.

I build clean, fast, mobile-ready websites tailored to your business. No bloated templates, no technical jargon, no agency price tags. Just a beautiful site that works — and brings in customers.

Whether you're a nail salon, a plumber, a bakery, or anything in between — if you don't have a website yet, let's change that.
